# Simple Custom Combination Guide:
You can experiment with all kinds of combinations using this method. Just make sure there's **enough sugar in the mix**, because that's what helps the microbes active. 
## Easy Sugar Sources:
- Banana peel
- Apple peel
- Peach peel
- Carrot peel
---
## Simple Ration Example (not tested)
```
1 whole banana peel + 1 whole apple peel + 1 small piece of lemon peel ( just the yellow part)
```
The nice thing is: even if the ratio isn't perfect, the method is forgiving. 
Worst case, you toss the tea bag and tweak the next batch.
Hope you have fun trying different mixes!
