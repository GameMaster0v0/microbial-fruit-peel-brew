# Fruit‑Peel Cold Brew Fertilizer  
*A zero‑waste, non‑commercial DIY method for gentle plant nutrition*

This repository documents a simple, clean, and low‑odor technique for making a **cold‑fermented fruit‑peel infusion** for plants. Unlike typical “banana peel tea,” this method avoids rot, slime, and bad smells by using:

- baked fruit peels  
- ground peel powder  
- a biodegradable tea bag  
- cold‑water steeping  
- a breathable cover  

The result is a **mild, microbially active infusion** that’s easy to make, consistent, and fully zero‑waste.

This method is shared for **personal, non‑commercial use only** under the **CC BY‑NC 4.0** license.

---

## 🌱 Why This Method Exists

Most DIY peel teas:
- rot quickly  
- smell unpleasant  
- grow anaerobic bacteria  
- produce unpredictable results  

This method fixes all of that by:
- sterilizing peels through baking  
- increasing surface area through grinding  
- containing solids in a tea bag  
- fermenting slowly in cold water  
- allowing airflow to prevent anaerobic conditions  

It’s closer to a **fruit‑peel kombucha for soil microbes** than a typical peel tea.

---

## ♻️ Zero‑Waste Philosophy

- The tea bag is biodegradable.  
- The contents break down easily in soil or compost.  
- No sludge, no mess, no leftover peel scraps.  

Everything returns to the earth.
